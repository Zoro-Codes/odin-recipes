Biriyani Recipe

Description

Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, spices, and meat or vegetables. It is known for its rich aroma and delicious flavor.

This recipe combines rice with marinated chicken, spices, and herbs to create a flavorful and satisfying meal.

Ingredients for Biryani

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken (or mutton)
  • 2 large onions (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 cup yogurt (curd)
  • 3-4 green chilies
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 4-5 cloves
  • 2-3 cardamom pods
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee

Steps to Make Biriyani

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan and fry sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  4. Add chicken, spices, and salt and cook for a few minutes.
  5. Add tomatoes and yogurt and cook until the chicken becomes tender.
  6. Boil the rice separately until it is partially cooked.
  7. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  8. Add coriander and mint leaves.
  9. Cover and cook on low heat for 10-15 minutes.
  10. Mix gently and serve hot.
